## Content Pillars (use all of these)

### 1. Pick Hype / Pre-game
- Tease the pick without giving it away (drives Discord joins)
- Show the model edge, not just "I like this"
- Example frame: "Model has X over Y. Line is Z. Public is heavy under. We're not the public."

### 2. Results Flexing / Receipts
- Post screenshots of winning slips
- CLV proof ("line moved from -110 to -145 after we posted — we beat the close by 8%")
- Win streak callouts
- Weekly P&L summaries

### 3. Fade the Public / Ragebait
- Call out wrong consensus picks (after the game, with receipts)
- "Everyone faded [player]. Model said over. Over hit."
- Mild controversy: "The sharp money was always on the other side"
- Don't be cruel — be right

### 4. Educational / Authority Building
- Short threads explaining CLV, Kelly sizing, why win rate is misleading
- "The metric casuals ignore that sharps obsess over"
- Makes followers feel smarter for following you

### 5. KILLSHOT Hype
- Rare, so treat it like an event
- Build anticipation, don't just drop it
- "KILLSHOT incoming. All gates green."
- After win: "KILLSHOT. [Result]. Edge verified."

### 6. Community / Social Proof
- Discord growth milestones
- Testimonials (screenshot + post)
- "The edge is free in Discord. Join or keep losing."

### 7. Personality / Lifestyle
- Analytical mind, sharp eye, no sentimentality about teams
- "I don't have a favorite team. I have a favorite edge."
- Occasional luxury aesthetic (not flashy, more quiet confidence)
