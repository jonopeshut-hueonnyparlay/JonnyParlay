## EMAIL 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** How to read a pick card in 60 seconds.

Every pick follows the same format. Here's what each field means.

**Example card:**
Jayson Tatum — PTS — OVER 27.5 (-115) — DraftKings
Proj: 29.8 | Line: 27.5 | Edge: 4.2% | Win Prob: 0.67 | Pick Score: 84 | Tier: Core

**Breaking it down:**

Proj: 29.8 | Line: 27.5
Our model projects Tatum at 29.8 points. The line is 27.5. That's a 2.3-point gap — that gap is the edge.

Edge: 4.2%
The probability difference between our model and the implied odds. Anything above 3% is actionable.

Win Prob: 0.67
Our model gives this a 67% chance of hitting. The book is pricing it closer to 54% (implied by -115 odds). That gap is where the value lives.

Pick Score: 84
A composite score (0-100) combining edge, win probability, matchup quality, and model confidence. 90+ qualifies as a KILLSHOT.

Tier: Core
This pick is visible to Core and above.

You don't need to understand the full model to use it. You need to understand what you're betting and why. This format tells you both.

## EMAIL 3 — Day 3
**Subject:** The one rule that separates long-run winners from everyone else.

Most bettors lose not because they pick wrong — but because they size wrong.

Here's the framework we use and recommend.

**Unit = 1% of your total bankroll**

Bankroll $1,000 → 1 unit = $10
Bankroll $5,000 → 1 unit = $50

**How picks are sized — driven by edge %:**

Edge 3–5%: 0.50u base
Edge 5–7%: 0.75u base
Edge 7–9%: 1.00u base
Edge 9%+:  1.25u base

Tier multipliers are applied on top — T1 picks carry full weight, lower tiers are scaled down. The model handles this automatically. Every pick card shows the final unit size.

**KILLSHOT sizing (separate system):**
- 3u default — pick score ≥90, all KILLSHOT gates cleared
- 4u max — win prob ≥0.70 AND edge ≥0.06 simultaneously

**Daily cap: 12 units total**
The model never posts more than 12u of exposure in a single session regardless of how many picks are on the card.

**The tilt rule:**
After 3 consecutive losses, stop for the day. Not because the model is broken — because your decision quality degrades when you're chasing.

**What this service provides:**
Best-available edge, game by game. What it does not provide: guaranteed outcomes. Edge compounds over hundreds of picks, not dozens. A losing week is normal. A losing month with positive CLV (closing line value) means the model is still working — variance ran against us, not the process.

Your job: size correctly, stay disciplined, let the edge compound.

— Jonny
